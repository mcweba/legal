2. Summary

Salty does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. The app works entirely offline and without an account. There is no server communication, no analytics or tracking services, and no advertising.

3. What Data Is Processed

Salty does not process any personal data. All inputs (e.g. pool dimensions, salt level) are processed exclusively in memory, locally on your device, and discarded when the app is closed.

One exception: a device setting (unit system, metric/imperial) is stored locally on your device (technically: AsyncStorage) so it persists across app launches. This setting never leaves your device, is not transmitted to anyone, and contains no personal information.

4. Sharing with Third Parties

No data is shared with third parties. The app contains no analytics, tracking, crash-reporting, or advertising SDKs.

5. Permissions

The app does not request any special device permissions (e.g. camera, location, contacts).

6. Your Rights

Since no personal data is processed or stored, there are no corresponding records to which access, correction, or deletion rights could apply. For questions, please reach out via the email address above.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

If the app's functionality changes and data processing is introduced in the future (e.g. a planned local pool profile or history feature, still without any server transmission), this privacy policy will be updated accordingly and the date above adjusted.
